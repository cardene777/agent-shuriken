# The pen technical illustration

The `pen-technical` finish of the `illustrated` family draws the usual explanatory figure of an article.
Its reference images are the images that `sample_ids` of the `pen-technical` profile name in `~/.styles/style-catalog.json`, kept as the original images.
Do not use comparison images made again from another figure as its reference.
Do not replace the lines and fills of the references with bold, comic-like enclosures.

## How it is drawn

- A white background. Thin to medium pen lines with a natural wobble, rubbing, and changes of pressure. Keep a black core in the main outlines, the letters, and the arrows, and put rubbing and gray hatching only in places. Lean neither to uniform bold comic outlines nor to drawing everything in pale gray pencil
- Draw small concrete objects with distinct shapes. Choose papers, safes, endpoints, records, and characters from their role in the article, and do not move a reference's props to another subject
- Color teal and orange with partial rubs of colored pencil or light layers. Light gray shadows and short hatching may be used. Do not surround the canvas with dark solid areas and bold color frames
- Place letters beside or under the objects, with the natural variation of handwriting. Do not let only the heading jump out in very bold type, and do not put each label in a rounded box or a speech bubble. Small color bands that tell states apart may be used
- Show relations with arrows and the shapes of records. Do not explain the point with poses or speech bubbles alone; let objects, connections, and letters read together
- Draw an AI agent as a cute non-human character, in the way the references draw one. A person in a reference is never a reason to add people to another figure

## Using the references and checking

Decide the objects and relations from the article and the content summary first, then apply the line, fill, background, and lettering conditions.
When the user's chosen original images are used as style references, record in the drawing instructions and the run record that they are inputs apart from the figure being drawn.
Do not mix the registries, safes, tokens, state names, or layout particular to a reference into the new figure.
Choose a layout that fits each figure's claim; the number of objects and the density need not match the reference.
The review opens the figure at full size and at the published width and compares the line quality, the coloring, and the balance of letters and objects with the references.
Using teal and orange, or drawing a cute robot, does not pass the style by itself.
