# The hand-drafted technical sketch

The `handdraft` finish of the `technical-sketch` family draws data structures, storage, conditions, amounts, and boundaries directly.
Its reference images are the images that `sample_ids` of the `handdraft` profile name in `~/.styles/style-catalog.json`, and its `reference_id` names the one the user chose as the standard of the drawing feel.
Choosing this finish chooses a style; it does not approve the layout of another figure or a candidate.

## How it is drawn

- A white background. Do not add mood with paper colors or shadows
- Dark black letters in a rounded natural handwriting. Do not drift toward a thin, pale script, and keep numbers dark and large enough to read when shrunk
- Outlines, dividers, and arrows in a clear black pen with a light wobble. Not marker lines much bolder than the letters, not mechanical hairlines, and not fuzzy pencil lines
- Ledgers, fields, brackets, shared axes, and connections show the conditions and amounts of that figure. Keep the text readable, and keep space when the elements are few. Do not stretch ledgers, text, or frames to fill the published width
- Color areas are light teal or orange hatching, limited to the ranges, amounts, and results that carry meaning. Gray hatching may support fields such as storage
- No filled heading bands, large rounded cards, heavy shadows, or uniform ornamental frames. Keep short color lines, brackets, and notes in tune with the black lines

## Content and checking

Never carry the deadlines, the two time axes, or the ledger layout of the reference image into a figure about something else.
Decide the objects and the layout of each figure from its article and content summary first, then pass the drawing conditions above in the drawing instructions.
When the user names the reference image as the style standard, pass the figure being drawn and the reference as separate images, and say that only the reference's lines, letters, fills, and background are used.
Record in the drawing instructions and the run record which input is redrawn and which is the style-only reference.
Reject the figure when the reference's time axes, ledgers, values, or number of objects appear in it.
In the review, open the figure at full size and at the published width next to the reference image, and check separately whether the letter darkness and shapes, the outlines, the light hatching, and the white ground match.
Also check the labels, numbers, conditions, and connections of the figure; matching the style never replaces passing the content.
