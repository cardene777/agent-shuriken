# The Superchain revenue share

An arrangement by which chains that joined the Superchain pass part of the revenue of the party that orders transactions (the sequencer) to the Optimism Collective.
Its form was set by the arrangement with Base: the larger of 2.5% of total revenue and 15% of profit.
Base announced in 2026-02 that it would move to a stack Base runs, and Base's share was to end.

| Point | What the official documents say |
|---|---|
| How much is passed | The larger of 2.5% of the sequencer's total revenue and 15% of the L2 profit after the cost of posting data to L1 |
| What Base got in return | Up to about 118 million OP (2.75% of the initial supply) over 6 years. The votes of this grant are capped at 9% of the total that can vote in one vote |
| The condition of the return | The OP grant was conditional on Base staying in the Superchain |
| Use of the collected amount | Optimism's blog of 2026-01-08 proposed buying back OP every month for one year with 50% of incoming Superchain revenue. It says about 5,868 ETH was collected over the past 12 months |
| The buyback | The answer on the governance forum of 2026-02-26 calls the buyback proposal approved |
| Base's share | The same answer says the earlier Superchain revenue share does not continue, as Base moves to a stack it runs itself |
| The relationship in its place | Base's blog says it continues with Optimism as an OP Enterprise customer, and the forum answer says the Optimism Foundation and Base made that arrangement. An answer of 2026-03-03 says OP Enterprise revenue goes to the Optimism Foundation and is not used for buybacks |

## Current status

The arrangement itself is live; the blog of 2026-01-08 said Base, Unichain, Ink, World Chain, Soneium, and OP Mainnet return part of their revenue.
The answer on the governance forum of 2026-02-26 said Base's earlier share does not continue.
That answer came from the `system` account, which carries the forum's operator mark, not from Base's account.
The same answer says that, since the grant was conditional on staying in the Superchain, Base cannot claim the part of the 2023 grant not yet received, and the 9% vote cap remains.

The 2023 post's "Base passes 15% of profit or 2.5% of revenue to Optimism" differs from the current state for Base.

## Not yet shipped

Whether the 2.5% and 15% formula still applies equally to every chain other than Base has not been checked in official documents.
Whether the mechanism that collects the share automatically on chain (the predeployed `FeeSplitter`) runs on mainnet has not been checked.
The relevant page of the developer documentation was not found.
The share of votes in favor of the buyback was seen only in reports and has not been checked on the governance forum's vote page.
