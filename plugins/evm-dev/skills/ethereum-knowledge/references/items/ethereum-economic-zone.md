# Ethereum Economic Zone (EEZ)

A framework that connects L1 and L2.
It aims to let the contracts of a rollup that joined the EEZ call contracts on Ethereum mainnet or on another EEZ rollup and use the returned value within the same transaction.
The announcement says processing across chains then passes or reverts as a whole like one transaction, liquidity is shared, and the security guarantee rests on Ethereum.

| Point | What the announcement says |
|---|---|
| Announcement | Published on 2026-03-29 by the official EEZ account and by Gnosis on the same day |
| Who builds it | Gnosis and Zisk are the launch builders, and the EEZ Association, based in Switzerland, owns development. Everything it builds is released as free software |
| Funding | The Ethereum Foundation provides it |
| Proofs | It uses Zisk's proving system. The announcement says progress in real-time proving made it possible |
| Gas | In most cases it can be paid in ETH wherever execution happens |
| Launch partners | Aave, Titan, Beaver Build, Centrifuge, and xStocks |

## Current status

The announcement said the technical architecture and protocol specification, performance measurements, details of the developer tools, and a path for existing protocols to use the EEZ would come out within a few weeks.
A search of the official announcements and of reports on 2026-11-08 found no specification or measurements.
The status therefore stays `announced`.
When a specification comes out, the status becomes `specified`, and the facts come from the specification instead of the announcement.
