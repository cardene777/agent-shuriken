# FpML 5.14 is the current Recommendation

ISDA released FpML 5.14 on 20 October 2026 and announced it on 28 October 2026; the FpML news archive lists it as the Recommendation, build 5.
It follows the 5.13 Recommendation of 12 May 2025.
The 5.14 additions include elections under version 2 of the 2002 ISDA Equity Derivatives Definitions, coverage of digital caps and floors and constant maturity swaps, and more flexible commodity schedules.

FpML is published in views (confirmation, reporting, recordkeeping, transparency, pretrade, and legal); a design names the view and the version each party uses, since many systems stay on an older 5.x.

For a design:

- The confirmation view describes the agreed economic terms; whether a confirmation is legally binding depends on the master agreement, not on the message
- Product terms in FpML can map to the CDM for lifecycle events; say which one the design treats as the source of truth
