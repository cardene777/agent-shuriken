# Geometry samples

This contract checks the real bounds of every visible object at sampled frames: each object stays in the safe area, objects overlap only where the overlap is allowed, connected objects meet edge to edge on a shared center line, and objects meant to hold still do not jitter.

## Where the samples come from

`out/review/geometry-samples.json` is generated by the same layout functions the renderer uses, the `frameLayoutAt()` that `remotion-explainer-production` builds.
Never write it by hand from screenshots.
The `layout` format of the `remotion-frame-checks` skill writes a `layout-samples.json` of another shape, so these samples keep their own name.

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `sourceHash` | The SHA-256 of the renderer and the layout source the samples came from |
| `viewport` | The video's `width` and `height`, and a `safeArea` of `left`, `right`, `top`, and `bottom` margins in pixels |
| `frames[]` | One entry per sampled frame: `frame`, `objects[]`, `connections[]`, and `allowedOverlaps[]` |
| `frames[].objects[]` | Every object visible at that frame, once: `id` and `bounds` (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`), and optionally `allowOutsideSafeArea: true` |
| `frames[].connections[]` | The `from` and `to` object IDs, the `fromEdge` and `toEdge` (`left`, `right`, `top`, or `bottom`), the `maxGap` between the two edges, and the `centerTolerance` between the two centers, in pixels and 0 when left out |
| `frames[].allowedOverlaps[]` | Pairs of object IDs that may overlap at that frame, such as an object and its shadow |
| `stableTracks[]` | Objects that must hold still: the `id`, the `frames` they are compared at, and the `maxDelta` in pixels, 0 when left out |

`examples/geometry-samples.json` shows a sorter, its inlet, and a parcel moving past them.

## What to sample

- Every visible object has exactly one bounds entry at each sampled frame
- Sample the entry, the turning points, the exit, and the camera transitions of every scene
- A connection joins the outer edges of two objects and lines them up on the center across the connection: for a `fromEdge` of `left` or `right`, the vertical centers are compared, and for `top` or `bottom`, the horizontal centers
- A stable track compares the `x`, `y`, `width`, and `height` at each of its frames with those at its first frame

## The check

Run the `check-geometry-samples` check of this skill on the file.

```bash
node <check-geometry-samples> out/review/geometry-samples.json
```

Each issue names its `rule`, the `frame`, and the `element` or the pair `a/b` it is about.

| Rule | Means |
|---|---|
| `viewport`, `frames` | The viewport has no positive width and height or no safe area, or no frame is sampled |
| `object-bounds`, `duplicate-object` | An object lacks an id or positive bounds, or has two bounds entries at one frame |
| `safe-area` | An object leaves the safe area without `allowOutsideSafeArea: true` |
| `overlap` | Two objects overlap by more than 0.5 square pixels and are not an allowed pair at that frame |
| `connection-object`, `connection-edge` | A connection names an object not sampled at that frame, or an edge other than `left`, `right`, `top`, or `bottom` |
| `connection-gap`, `connection-alignment` | The edges are further apart than `maxGap`, or the centers differ by more than `centerTolerance` |
| `stable-track-samples`, `stable-track-jitter` | A track lacks positive bounds at one of at least two frames, or moves by more than `maxDelta` from its first frame |
