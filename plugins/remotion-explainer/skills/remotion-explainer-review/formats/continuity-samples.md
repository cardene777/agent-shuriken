# Continuity samples

Scenes are not separate slides.
At every scene boundary, record how objects, states, and the camera are handed over by cause, and check it in sampled frames.

## Where the samples come from

`out/review/continuity-samples.json` is generated by the same layout functions the renderer uses, the `frameLayoutAt()` that `remotion-explainer-production` builds.
Never write it by hand from screenshots.

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `schemaVersion` | `1` |
| `sourceHash` | The SHA-256 of the renderer source the samples came from |
| `boundaries[]` | One entry per scene boundary: `id`, `frames` (`before`, `during`, `after`), `camera`, `persistent[]`, and `handoffs[]` |
| `boundaries[].camera` | `maxCenterDelta`, how far in pixels the camera center may move in one step, and `maxScaleDelta`, how much its scale may change in one step |
| `boundaries[].persistent[]` | Objects that stay under the same `id` at `before` and `after`, with their own `maxCenterDelta` and `maxScaleDelta` |
| `boundaries[].handoffs[]` | A transform or a move: the object `from` at `before` passes through `via` at `during` and becomes `to` at `after`, with `maxCenterDelta`, `maxScaleDelta`, and the `reason`, the cause in words |
| `frames[]` | Each sampled frame: `frame`, `camera` (`x`, `y`, `scale`), and `objects[]` with `id` and `bounds` (`x`, `y`, `width`, `height`) |

Every limit is 0 when left out.
`examples/continuity-samples.json` shows a parcel that leaves the warehouse shelf and rides onto the sorter belt while the warehouse stays.

## Rules for a boundary

- A boundary has samples at its `before`, `during`, and `after` frames, and a camera at all three
- The camera does not reset across a boundary; a fixed camera records the same values at all three frames
- A boundary carries at least one persistent object or one handoff
- Every handoff states its cause in `reason`

## How the check measures

| Measure | Compared with |
|---|---|
| Camera | The distance between the camera centers from `before` to `during` and from `during` to `after`, against `camera.maxCenterDelta`, and the change of `scale` on the same two steps, against `camera.maxScaleDelta` |
| Persistent object | The distance between the centers of its bounds at `before` and at `after`, against `maxCenterDelta`, and its change of size, against `maxScaleDelta` |
| Handoff | The same two measures from `from` to `via` and from `via` to `to` |

The change of size between two bounds is the larger of `|width1 / width2 - 1|` and `|height1 / height2 - 1|`.

## The check

Run the `check-continuity-samples` check of this skill on the file.

```bash
node <check-continuity-samples> out/review/continuity-samples.json
```

Each issue names its `rule` and the `boundary` it is about.

| Rule | Means |
|---|---|
| `boundaries` | The file lists no boundary |
| `boundary-frames` | The `before`, `during`, or `after` frame has no sample |
| `camera-data` | The boundary has no camera limits, or one of its three samples has no camera `x`, `y`, and `scale` |
| `camera-jump`, `camera-scale-jump` | The camera resets or jumps, or its scale jumps, beyond the limits |
| `nothing-carried` | The boundary has no persistent object and no handoff |
| `persistent-missing`, `persistent-jump`, `persistent-scale-jump` | A persistent object lacks bounds at `before` or `after`, moves too far, or changes size too abruptly |
| `handoff-chain`, `handoff-reason` | A handoff lacks one of `from`, `via`, and `to` at its frame, or has no `reason` |
| `handoff-jump`, `handoff-scale-jump` | A handoff moves too far or changes size too abruptly in one step |
