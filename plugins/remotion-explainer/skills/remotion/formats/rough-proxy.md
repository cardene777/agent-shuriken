# Rough proxy

The moving composition rough uses proxies made for it instead of the finished art, each one readable by its outline and its operation.
A proxy is not a worse copy of the finished art.
It is a separate stand-in visual language for checking time, cause, and composition first.

## What the rough leaves out

The rough may simplify only the texture, the shading, and the detail of the materials of the art.
The composition, the framing, the camera moves, the order of appearance, the routes of objects, transforms, branches, comparisons, the guiding of the eye, and the handoffs between scenes are built exactly as in the final cut, as the `choreography` format of the `remotion-motion-plan` skill describes.
The rough never leaves out an element that first appears in the final cut, or a discovery of camera or composition that only the final cut has.
A plan whose understanding, density, or cause does not hold in the rough goes back; adding the finished art will not make it hold.

## Each proxy

Define for each proxy its `role`, `silhouette`, `port`, `state`, `operation`, and `scale`.

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `role` | What the object does in the explanation |
| `silhouette` | The outline that tells it apart without text |
| `port` | Where connections and handoffs attach to it |
| `state` | The states it passes through |
| `operation` | What is done to it |
| `scale` | Its size relative to the other proxies |

## The two-second test

Without any text, the main object, its counterpart, the carried object, and the change of state must read within two seconds.
When they do not, remake the proxy; do not add lines, tags, glows, or finished PNGs.
Collect the tests in `out/review/rough-proxy-studies/index.html` of the project, and never hand over single MP4 files as the place to review them.
In the review HTML of a silent rough, give every video `autoplay muted loop playsinline`, and set `defaultPlaybackRate` and `playbackRate` to 1.5 when the page loads.

## Not allowed

- A main object made of generic rectangles, circles, lines, and labels
- Finished felt art, final PNGs, or generated images that assume the finished look, anywhere in the rough

Only after the full rough is approved is each proxy replaced with finished art that keeps the same role and operation.
