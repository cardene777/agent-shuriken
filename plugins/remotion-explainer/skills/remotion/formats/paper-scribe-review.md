# Paper scribe review

Review the entry, operation, read, and handoff frames, and every boundary frame, of a video in the paper-scribe language of `formats/paper-scribe.md`.

1. Does the camera move because the carry object or the next proof needs a new focus, rather than because the scene needs motion?
2. Is every paper structure attached to a named object and a concrete task of reading? Reject decorative borders and generic cards
3. Does the outline reveal a meaningful structural contour before the PNG paint settles? Reject automatic silhouette tracing and paint that appears at once
4. Is the subject object introduced only when it has a causal role? Reject a finished board, or a row of three objects, at the entry of a beat
5. Does one state or object stay, move, or transform across the boundary, and does it motivate the next camera focus?
6. Is there no arrow where the object itself shows the route? Where a connector stays, does its whole path lie outside the PNGs, the annotations, and the movement lanes in every reviewed frame?
