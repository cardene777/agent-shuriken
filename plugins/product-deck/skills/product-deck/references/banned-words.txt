# Words that must not appear on a slide. One word per line; from `#` to the end of a line is a note.
# `audit.mjs` reads this file by default and flags a slide where a word appears.
# The words differ per deck: pass another list with `--words <path>`, and add the words a deck
# decided against (see writing-rules.md, "Do not call one thing by different words") to that list.
#
# The words come from writing-rules.md. Each was pointed out in a real deck as reading like
# machine output or making no sense; none was listed by guess. They are Japanese, because the
# checks of these rules read Japanese text.

# --- Words that leave the reader to fill in what they point at ---
# "the part that was done" is plain to the writer, and reads as an amount or as a share to the reader
した分
た分が
た分も
た分は
置き場所だけ
作る量
# Words that point at a place or a time without saying which.
# A slide is read on its own, so pointing at the previous slide or line does not reach the reader
その場に
その場で
ここで
ここに
こちらで
こちらが
この時の
そこで止

分かれ道はここ
関門
が居る場所
