# Instructions to hand to another reader

Give only this file and the script to a reader who does not carry over the conversation that wrote it.
Give the reader the following text as it is.

---

Listen to the following script as if you were reading it aloud line by line.
You are looking for lines that do not sound like people actually talking.
The targets are lines that sound like someone reading a script, or reading written text aloud.

The points to check are as follows.

- Whether the speakers talk by taking up each other's words. Whether a speaker is only reading the sentences of their own turn, unrelated to the previous line.
- Whether back-channel words, repeats of the other person's words, and 「ね」「よ」 appear about as often as when people talk.
- Whether it has turned into a form where one person plays the expert and the other poses questions.
- Whether it asserts too strongly. Whether hearsay is closed with 「ようです」 ("it seems") or 「ということです」 ("it is said that").
- Whether contractions (「ちゃう」「じゃあ」) or written-language forms (「である」「であり、」) are mixed in.
- Whether a sentence breaks off midway at a particle. Whether a question drops 「か」.
- Whether the two agree on everything and only say the same things to each other.
- Whether the same phrasing or the same sentence ending keeps repeating. Whether a responding line starts with the same back-channel word every time.
- Whether a line restates, in almost the same words, what the other person just said or what was read out.
- Whether 「ね」 or 「よね」 is attached to every line.
- Whether empty words such as 「所」「こと」「ある」 pile up in one line.
- Whether a comparison of the same type as in the previous topic is repeated.
- Whether the close of a topic takes up what the other person said just before.

Return one line for each line that has a problem, in this form.

```
L<line number> <how it sounds> → <how to say it so it sounds like people actually talking>
```

When there is no problem, return only `なし` ("none").
Do not add facts, numbers, or names that are not in the script to your suggested fixes.
