What this page is

Only service names, URLs and dates were collected from your password manager (such as 1Password) and the logins saved in your browser. No passwords were read.

What happens next

Starting with the services you marked OK, the AI checks for paid plans, cancels any it finds, and deletes the account.

You complete two-factor checks, and sign in yourself wherever the AI cannot fill in your login.
